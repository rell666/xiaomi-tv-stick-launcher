# Space photograph credits

The fifteen "Space" wallpapers (ids `space-*`) and the images derived from them (thumbnails, panel
backdrops and the `space-*` files in `materials/`) come from NASA, ESA/Webb and ESA/Hubble.

- **ESA/Webb and ESA/Hubble images** are released under the
  [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)
  (<https://esawebb.org/copyright/>, <https://esahubble.org/copyright/>, linked from each image page).
  Credit them as shown below.
- **NASA images** are not subject to copyright in the United States
  (<https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/>). Each one was checked on its
  images.nasa.gov record: credited to NASA, with no third-party photographer and no "courtesy of"
  or copyright line. NASA does not endorse this app.

Changes: each image was cropped to 3:2 and resized to 1920x1280; the thumbnail, panel backdrop and
blurred or tinted material images were derived from that. Each source page was read and each
original downloaded on 2026-10-31. Original file URLs, sizes and SHA-256 are in
[wallpapers/SPACE-SOURCES.json](wallpapers/SPACE-SOURCES.json), the machine-readable record.
