# Cyberpunk photograph credits

The fifteen "Cyberpunk" wallpapers (ids `cyberpunk-*`) and the images derived from them (thumbnails,
panel backdrops and the `cyberpunk-*` files in `materials/`) are photographs from Wikimedia Commons
(<https://commons.wikimedia.org/>), each under the licence shown below. Each photographer keeps
their copyright; none of them endorses this app.

- **CC BY** images may be used with credit to the author: credit them as shown below.
- **CC BY-SA** images may be used with credit, and what is made from them is shared under the same
  licence: the cropped wallpaper and every image derived from it are released under the same
  CC BY-SA version as the original.
- **CC0** images were dedicated to the public domain by their authors; they are credited anyway.

Each file page's licence section was read on 2026-11-01: none carries a personality-rights or
trademark warning, and each Flickr import has passed Commons' Flickr licence review.

Changes: each image was cropped to 3:2 and resized to 1920x1280; the thumbnail, panel backdrop and
blurred or tinted material images were derived from that. Each original was downloaded on
2026-11-01. Original file URLs, sizes and SHA-256 are in
[wallpapers/CYBERPUNK-SOURCES.json](wallpapers/CYBERPUNK-SOURCES.json), the machine-readable record.
